# Expense Reimbursement & Spending Rules

## Sec 1: Eligible Expense Categories & Caps
Employees may claim reimbursement for authorized business expenses within the following monthly/per-incident limits:
- **Internet & Home Office Utility:** Up to 2,000 BDT per month with a valid ISP invoice.
- **Domestic Business Travel:** Actual economy airfare/train fare plus a per-diem meal allowance of 1,500 BDT per day.
- **Client Meals & Entertainment:** Up to 4,000 BDT per client meeting without prior executive sign-off.
- **Software & Hardware Accessories:** Requires valid tax invoice and asset registration.

## Sec 2: Submission & Receipt Guidelines
- All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date with itemized receipts.
- Personal expenses, traffic fines, and non-business entertainment are strictly non-reimbursable.

## Sec 3: Auto-Approval vs. Manager Approval Threshold
- Claims up to **5,000 BDT** are eligible for automated processing if within category limits.
- **High-Risk Escalation Rule:** Any expense reimbursement claim exceeding **5,000 BDT** requires explicit human manager approval before disbursement.

## Sec 4: Anti-Splitting Compliance Policy
- Employees are strictly prohibited from splitting a single transaction or invoice into multiple smaller claims to bypass the 5,000 BDT auto-approval threshold. Linked same-day claims from the same employee are aggregated automatically and flagged for audit.
