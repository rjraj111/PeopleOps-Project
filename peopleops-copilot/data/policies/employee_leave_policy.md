# Employee Leave Policy

## Sec 1: Leave Quotas & Eligibility
All full-time employees are entitled to the following paid leave quotas per calendar year:
- **Annual Leave:** 15 days per year, accrued monthly. Unused annual leave up to 5 days may be carried forward to the next year.
- **Casual Leave:** 10 days per year for urgent personal matters. Cannot be carried forward.
- **Sick Leave:** 14 days per year for medical illness or recovery.
- **Parental Leave:** 16 weeks of paid maternity leave and 2 weeks of paid paternity leave.

## Sec 2: Medical Certificate Requirements
- Sick leave up to 2 consecutive days does not require a medical certificate.
- Sick leave of **3 or more consecutive days** requires a valid registered physician's medical certificate submitted upon return.

## Sec 3: Notice Period & Manager Approval Rules
- Annual leave requires at least **7 days' prior notice** to the employee's direct line manager.
- Casual leave requires at least 24 hours' notice except in genuine emergencies.
- **High-Risk Escalation Rule:** Any single leave request exceeding **3 consecutive days** is flagged as high-risk and requires formal manager approval before confirmation.
