# Medical & Wellness Benefits Guide

## Sec 1: Outpatient & Hospitalization Coverage
- Full-time employees receive annual outpatient medical reimbursement up to 25,000 BDT for doctor consultations, diagnostic tests, and prescribed medicines.
- Inpatient hospitalization coverage is provided up to 300,000 BDT per year under the corporate group health insurance plan.

## Sec 2: Eligible Dependents
- Coverage extends to the employee's spouse and up to two dependent children under 21 years of age.
- Parents may be added under the optional co-pay rider during annual enrollment.

## Sec 3: Excluded Treatments & Filing Deadlines
- Cosmetic procedures, elective dental whitening, and non-prescribed supplements are excluded from coverage.
- Medical claims must be filed within 45 days of hospital discharge or consultation date.
